BLALOW DATA DELETION INSTRUCTIONS

Last updated: 22 October 2026

You can ask us to delete the information we hold about you from messages with @blalowclothing.

How to ask

Do either of these:

  1. Send us a DM on Instagram saying "Delete my data" from the account you used to message us.
  2. Email blalow.co@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include your Instagram username and, if you ordered, the name and phone number you used.

We may ask you to confirm it is really you before we delete anything, so nobody else can delete your information.

What we will delete

  • Your name, delivery address, phone number and order details that we hold, including the order message in our team's group chat (WhatsApp or Telegram), except what we must keep by law.
  • Technical logs that include your messages or your Instagram ID, where we can find them. These are kept for 30 days in any case.

What we have to keep

Records of completed orders and payments (such as invoices) may need to be kept for tax and accounting rules for about 7 years. We will keep only what the law requires and delete the rest.

What we do not control

  • Your Instagram messages themselves belong to Instagram (Meta). You can delete your side of the conversation in the Instagram app, and Meta's own tools and policies cover what Meta keeps.
  • Our automated assistant keeps only a short memory of your conversation in the temporary memory of our server. It is not stored in a database and disappears when the server restarts.
  • Our AI service provider and delivery partners process data under their own terms. We will pass on your request to them where we are able to.

How long it takes

We aim to complete your request within 30 days and will confirm to you when it is done.
