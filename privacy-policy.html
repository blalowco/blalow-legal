BLALOW PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you send a message to @blalowclothing on Instagram, how our automated assistant uses it, and your choices.

Who we are

Blalow ("we", "us") runs the Instagram account @blalowclothing and sells clothing from Chennai, India. You can contact us at blalow.co@gmail.com.

Our automated assistant

When you send a direct message (DM) to @blalowclothing, an automated assistant may reply. It tells you it is an AI assistant, answers questions about our products, prices and sizes, and collects the details needed for an order. A member of our team confirms every order, sets the final price including delivery, and takes payment details from there. You can ask for a person at any time.

What we collect

  • Your Instagram account's numeric ID, which Instagram sends to us with each message.
  • The text of your messages, and a note that you sent a photo, shared post or voice note (the assistant cannot see or hear these).
  • If you place an order: your name, delivery address, PIN code, phone number, and what you want to buy.
  • Payment: you pay by UPI to our account. We see the payment screenshot you send. We never ask for card numbers, passwords or one-time codes in chat, and the assistant will not accept them.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and answer your questions.
  • To take, confirm, pay for and ship your order, and to give you support afterwards.
  • To keep our service safe, for example to stop spam and abuse.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Meta Platforms (Instagram): your messages travel through Instagram. Their handling of your messages is covered by Instagram's own privacy policy.
  • An AI service provider: to write replies, the text of your conversation is sent to a third-party AI model provider (currently Anthropic). It processes the text to generate a reply, under its own terms. We do not use your messages to train AI models ourselves.
  • Our team, who read the messages that need a person, for example to confirm your order. When you confirm an order, your name, phone number, delivery address and order details are also posted to our team's internal WhatsApp group, through a messaging service provider that connects our assistant to WhatsApp, so that we can check stock and prepare your order. WhatsApp is operated by Meta.
  • Delivery and logistics partners (for example Shiprocket and couriers) receive your name, address and phone number so your order can be shipped.
  • Banks and payment providers process your UPI payment.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • The assistant keeps a short memory of your current conversation only so it can follow what you are saying. This is held temporarily in the memory of our server. It is not saved in a database and is discarded when the server restarts.
  • To keep the assistant quiet while a person is handling your chat, our server saves your Instagram ID with a pause timer for up to 24 hours after a team member replies. No message text is saved.
  • Messages remain in your Instagram inbox until you or Instagram remove them.
  • Order records (name, address, phone, items and payment confirmation), including the order message in our team WhatsApp group, are kept for as long as needed to fulfil your order and meet our legal, tax and accounting duties, for about 7 years, and then deleted.
  • Technical logs are kept for 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. See our data deletion instructions at https://blalowco.github.io/blalow-legal/data-deletion.html. You can also stop messaging the assistant at any time and ask for a person instead. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, and you may complain to your local privacy authority.

Security

We limit who can see order details, and the assistant is set up not to share payment details other than our UPI ID. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

Our service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

International processing

Instagram, our AI provider and our tools may process data in other countries, including countries outside India.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
